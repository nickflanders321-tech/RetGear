WoW Forever Mining Leveling Guide 1-300 with Best Routes
This WoW Forever Mining leveling guide will show you the fastest way to level Mining from 1 to 300. You will also find every trainer location and where to train Mining past 75, 150 and 225.
1-65
120-140x Smelt Copper until you reach 65. Keep 70 Copper Bars, you will need them for Bronze.
65-75
Make 70x Smelt Tin . It turns grey at 75, but you need all 70 Tin Bars for Bronze anyway.
75-105
Make 70x Smelt Bronze from the Copper Bars and Tin Bars you made. You will end up somewhere between 100 and 110, so you might need a few more.
105-120
Learn Smelt Silver from your trainer. Check the price of Silver Ore and Silver Bar first, because this part can get expensive. If you don't mind the cost, make 35-45x Smelt Silver until you reach 120. The last 5 points to 125 could take around 50 more Silver Ore, so I recommend mining from 120. (or keep smelting if you have the gold)

125-175
At 155 you can learn Smelt Gold from your trainer. It gives a point every time until 170, so around 22 Gold Ore takes you from 155 to 175 and you can start on Mithril right away. I recommend checking the price of Gold Ore and Gold Bar first.
