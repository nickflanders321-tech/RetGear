1-65
140x Smelt Copper until you reach 65. Keep 70 Copper Bars, you will need them for Bronze.
65-75
70x Smelt Tin . It turns grey at 75, but you need all 70 Tin Bars for Bronze anyway.
75-105
70x Smelt Bronze from the Copper Bars and Tin Bars you made. You will end up somewhere between 100 and 110.
105-120
43x Smelt Silver until you reach 120. Check the price of Silver Ore and Silver Bar first, because this part can get expensive.
120-125
Mine from 120 to 125 (see 65-125 below). Smelt Silver rarely gives a point after 120, so these 5 points could take around 60 more Silver Ore. (or keep smelting if you have the gold)
125-138
20x Smelt Iron . You will probably reach around 138.
138-155
From 140 to 155 no smelting recipe gives skill points, so you have to mine. Mine Iron Deposits until 155 (see 125-175 below).
155-175
25x Smelt Gold . You will probably reach around 177, so you can start on Mithril right away (see 175-245 below).
