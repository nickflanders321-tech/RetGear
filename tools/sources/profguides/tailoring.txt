WoW Forever Tailoring Leveling Guide 1-300

Apprentice Tailoring (1-55)
You will need 28 Coarse Thread for the 1-55 part. You can buy the threads and dyes from the Tailoring Supply vendor near your trainer. There is none next to the trainer in Tirisfal Glades, but you can buy the Coarse Thread a short walk away, in Brill from Abigail Shiel .
