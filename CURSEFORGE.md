# Waypath+

**Level 1 to 60 in WoW Forever without alt-tabbing.** An arrow that points to your next quest, a route for every race, class and faction, a clean quest tracker, and gear upgrades for your spec. Professions, fishing, gold and a collectible card game come along for the ride.

> **Ready for launch day.** Built on WoW Forever's own quest data and updated within hours when Blizzard patches the game or the quest data changes. Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and Shen'dralas are already in the route, and their steps fill in on their own as soon as the live quest data is out. English game clients (enUS/enGB). Found something odd? Type `/wp report`.

---

## Leveling and the arrow
- **Your route, 1 to 60, Horde and Alliance:** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead, Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome and Skyborne each start in their own zone, then follow their faction's route to 60, with the dungeons to run on the way.
- **The arrow:** double-click any quest, step, quest giver, trainer, flight master or farming spot and the arrow takes you there. It also follows the world map, your quest log, the minimap (Ctrl-click) and Questie icons.
- **One clear action per quest:** *Pick up* from whom, *Do* with your progress, *Hand in* to whom, or *Later* and what opens it. Steps tick themselves off.
- **Quest objectives on the world map** as gold "..." bubbles; click one to point the arrow at it.
- **Turn-in runs:** with several quests ready, one click sends the arrow to each hand-in, nearest first.
- **Smart route:** the tracker picks the zone that suits your level, where you are and what's already in your quest log, and names a couple of good alternatives.
- **Quests accepted and turned in for you** (hold Shift to pause), with the best reward picked for your spec.
- **Class quests** for every class (including WoW Forever's new ones) and the quests to collect before each dungeon.
- **Travel tips and flight paths:** the quickest way to your next zone with hearthstone, flight paths, zeppelins and boats, plus every flight master, nearest first.
- **Never lost:** if you die, the arrow leads you back to your body.

## Gear for your class and spec
- **Upgrade scores on every tooltip,** weighted for your class and spec, with green arrows on upgrades in your bags and an Equip button when you loot one.
- **A gear guide for every class:** what to collect at each level and exactly where it comes from (dungeon and boss, or quest, zone and level).
- **2nd spec, any class, any spec:** its gear list, tooltip scores and a one-click gear swap.

## Professions, fishing and gold
- **All twelve professions, 1-300,** with the materials you still need, recipes, trainers, vendors and farming spots. Ore and herb alerts when you walk near a node that still gives skill.
- **Fishing:** where to fish for your skill, poles and lures, a lure timer, fish per hour, and an immersive mode that fades your interface while you fish.
- **Auction House prices** from a background scan that never blocks your searches (Auctionator and TSM prices are used too). Items worth more on the AH than at a vendor are flagged and kept.
- **Bank alts:** any character can collect materials for your main. Bags sorted in one click, junk sold and gear repaired at vendors.

## The tracker
Your quests by zone with level colors, progress and distance; new quests nearby; your route; class quests, the next dungeon, your next upgrade and reminders in one tidy "More" row. In a party: reminders for blessings and auras, alerts when someone needs a cleanse or is about to die, and a run report after every dungeon.

## Bonus: Waypath Cards
Every item, NPC, place and player you meet becomes a card, with its own picture: 1,300+ cards, four finishes, booster packs earned by playing (two free packs a day in launch week, plus a launch gift), and the Waypath Arena to duel the computer or a friend in your party, with ranked seasons and a realm leaderboard. Not here to level? "Cards only" mode turns the guide off and keeps just the cards (`/wp cardsonly on`).

### Getting started
Install, log in, and a short setup asks how much help you want (Full guidance, Balanced or Minimal) and which professions you'll level. Type `/wp` or click the minimap button. `/wp help` lists every command.

### Bugs and ideas
`/wp report` gives you a copy-ready report with your last error and a link to the right form on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/nickflanders321-tech/RetGear/issues): reports from the first days of launch are fixed fastest.

### Language
Made for English game clients: Waypath+ matches quest, zone and profession names in English.
