# Waypath+

**The all-in-one companion for WoW Forever, for every class and both factions.** A GPS-style arrow and route from level 1 to 60, a clean quest tracker, gear upgrades, a 2nd spec for every class, all twelve professions, fishing, gold, and a collectible card game built from the world itself.

> **Beta.** English game clients (enUS/enGB). Please report anything odd: type `/wp errors`, copy the text and post it on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/nickflanders321-tech/RetGear/issues).

---

## Leveling and the GPS
- **A route for every race, Horde and Alliance:** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead, Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome and Skyborne each start in their own zone, then follow the shared route to 60.
- **The arrow:** double-click any quest, step, quest giver, trainer, flight master or farming spot and the arrow takes you there. It also follows the world map, your quest log, the minimap (Ctrl-click) and Questie icons.
- **Quest objectives on the world map** as the same gold "..." bubbles the quest log uses; click one to point the arrow at it.
- **One clear action per quest:** *Pick up* from whom, *Do* with your progress, *Hand in* to whom, or *Later* and what opens it. Steps tick themselves off.
- **Turn-in runs:** with several quests ready, one click sends the arrow to each hand-in, nearest first.
- **Quests accepted and turned in for you** (hold Shift to pause), with the best reward picked.
- **Class quests** for every class and the quests to collect before each dungeon.
- **Travel tips and flight paths:** the quickest way to your next zone with hearthstone, flight paths, zeppelins and boats, plus a list of every flight master (nearest first).

## Gear
- **Upgrade scores on every tooltip,** weighted for your class and spec, with green arrows on upgrades in your bags and an Equip button when you loot one.
- **A gear guide for every class:** what to collect at each level and exactly where it comes from; click any item for the dungeon and boss, or the quest, zone and level.
- **2nd spec, any class, any spec:** its gear list, tooltip scores and a one-click gear swap when you change spec.

## Professions, fishing and gold
- **All twelve professions, 1-300,** with the exact materials still needed, recipes, trainers, vendors and farming spots (double-click one for the arrow). Ore and herb alerts when you walk near a node that still gives skill.
- **Immersive fishing:** cast and your interface fades away, leaving the water, a Cast button and a small display of your skill, cast timer and catches. Plus fishing spots, lure timer and fish per hour.
- **Auction House prices** from a background scan that never blocks your searches; items worth more on the AH than at a vendor are flagged and kept. Auctionator and TSM prices are used too.
- **Profiles or bank alts:** every character can have its own profile or become a bank alt that collects mats for your main. Bags sorted in one click, junk sold and gear repaired at vendors.

## Waypath Cards: a collectible card game made from the world
Every item, NPC, place and raider you meet becomes a card, and every card has its own picture.
- **1,300+ cards** in four kinds: Items (their own icons), NPCs (their 3D models, moving), Places (hand-painted landscapes for all 376) and Raiders, the players you group with, shown as a character in raid gear.
- **Three kinds of pack:** Pack (5 cards), Jumbo (8) and Super Jumbo (12); every card can come out of every pack. Packs are free during the beta. A booster-pack opening screen with a spotlit pack, a tear-open and a card-by-card reveal.
- **Four finishes** for every card: Common, Rare, Holo and Full art.
- **Earn coins and packs by playing:** XP, level-ups, quests, kills and skill points, plus level milestones (10, 20, 30, 40, 45, 50, 55 to 59), dungeon boss clears, the last quest of a quest line, gear upgrades, and a big present for reaching level 60: five packs of each size and three guaranteed cards of 105 power or more.
- **Collect:** pages for Collection, Sets, Today, Trade and Ledger; progress bars on every set, a showcase, link cards in chat, trade and offer cards to your group, and sell extras.
- **Waypath Arena:** build a deck and duel the computer with the cards you own, or duel another Waypath+ player, with a ranked ladder and a versus splash screen. Opening hand mulligan, mana crystals, taunt, charge, rush, shield, freeze, stealth and spells.
- `/wp cards` opens the collection; `/wp cardcheck` tests every card picture in your game.

## Done for you
- **Smart tracker** on the right: your quests by zone with level colors, progress and distance, new quests nearby, your route, class quests, dungeon, upgrade, professions and reminders in one tidy "More" row.
- **In a party:** reminders for blessings and auras, alerts when someone needs a cleanse or is about to die, and a run report after every dungeon.
- **Quiet error catcher:** `/wp errors` gives you a copy-ready report.

### Getting started
Install, log in and a short setup wizard asks how much help you want (Full guidance, Balanced or Minimal) and which professions you'll level. Type `/wp` or click the minimap button to open the window. `/wp help` lists every command.

### Language
Waypath+ matches quest, zone and profession names in English, so it's made for English game clients. Card names in other scripts (Chinese, Korean, Cyrillic) display correctly.
