# Waypath+

**An all-in-one leveling companion for WoW Forever (Horde).** Your route with an arrow, a clean quest tracker, talents, gear upgrades, a 2nd spec for every class, all twelve professions, fishing and gold, in one add-on.

> **Beta.** English game clients (enUS/enGB). Please report anything odd: type `/wp errors`, copy the text and post it on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/nickflanders321-tech/RetGear/issues).

---

## Leveling
- **A route for every Horde race:** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead and Skyborne each start in their own zone, then follow the shared Horde route to 60.
- **One clear action per quest:** the tracker tells you exactly what to do next: *Pick up* from whom, *Do* with your progress, *Hand in* to whom, or *Later* and what opens it. Steps tick themselves off as you turn quests in.
- **The arrow:** double-click any quest, step, quest giver, trainer or farming spot and the arrow takes you there.
- **Turn-in runs:** with several quests ready, one click sends the arrow to each turn-in, nearest first.
- **Class quests** for every class, including Forever's new ones, and the quests to collect before each dungeon.
- **Travel tips:** the quickest way to your next zone using your hearthstone, flight paths, zeppelins and boats.

## Gear and talents
- **Upgrade scores on every tooltip,** weighted for your class and spec, with green arrows on upgrades in your bags and an Equip button when you loot one.
- **A gear guide for every class:** what to collect at each level and exactly where it comes from. Click any item to see the dungeon and boss, or the quest, its zone and level.
- **Talents for every class:** a leveling build from current WoW Forever guides, what to work toward and where your next point goes.
- **2nd spec, any class, any spec:** pick Holy, Protection, Restoration, Feral, anything; get its gear list, tooltip scores and a one-click gear swap.

## Professions
- **All twelve professions, 1-300**, using the best leveling routes available.
- **What you still need:** the exact number of each material left for your current step, with recipes, trainers and vendors.
- **Where to go:** farming, gathering and skinning spots near you. Double-click one and the arrow takes you there.
- **Ore and herb alerts** when you walk near a node that still gives skill.

## Fishing
- **Immersive fishing:** cast and your interface fades away, leaving the water, a big Cast button and a small display with your skill, a cast timer, your catches and any whispers. Move, press Esc or Enter to bring it back.
- **A loud bobber splash** (music and ambience muted while you fish) and a "cast again" flash when a fish gets away.
- **Fishing spots** for your skill, lure timer, fish per hour and raw fish to cook.

## Gold and alts
- **Auction House prices** from a fast background scan that never blocks your own searches; items worth more on the AH than at a vendor are flagged.
- **Profiles or bank alts:** every new character can have its own profile, or become a bank alt that collects mats for your main.

---

### Getting started
Install, log in and a short setup wizard asks how much help you want (Full guidance, Balanced or Minimal) and which professions you'll level. Type `/wp` or click the minimap button to open the window. `/wp help` lists every command.

### Language
Waypath+ matches quest, zone and profession names in English, so it's made for English game clients.
