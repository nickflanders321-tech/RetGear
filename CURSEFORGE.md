# Waypath+

**Level 1 to 60 in WoW Forever without alt-tabbing.** An arrow that points to your next quest, a route for every race, class and faction, a clean quest tracker and gear upgrades for your spec. Professions leveled the cheapest way, fishing, gold, and a collectible card game with its own Arena come along for the ride.

> **Kept up with WoW Forever.** Built on the game's own quest data and updated automatically within hours when Blizzard patches the game or the quest data changes: new dungeons, hotfixes and quests land in the route on their own. Found something odd? Type `/wp report`.

---

## Leveling and the arrow
- **Your route, 1 to 60, Horde and Alliance:** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead, Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome and Skyborne each start in their own zone, then follow their faction's route to 60, with the dungeons to run on the way (and where every dungeon door is, including the new ones).
- **The arrow:** double-click any quest, step, quest giver, trainer, flight master or farming spot and the arrow takes you there. It also follows the world map, your quest log, the minimap (Ctrl-click) and Questie icons.
- **One clear action per quest:** *Pick up* from whom, *Do* with your progress, *Hand in* to whom, or *Later* and what opens it. Steps tick themselves off.
- **A route that adapts to you.** The tracker picks the zone that suits your level, where you stand and what's already in your quest log. Within a zone, a step where you can hand in or that's right where you are comes first, so there's less walking back and forth. Grey quests never hold you up.
- **Quests accepted and turned in for you** (hold Shift to pause), with the best reward picked for your spec. Repeatable hand-ins are always left to you.
- **Class quests** for every class (including WoW Forever's new ones) and the quests to collect before each dungeon.
- **Travel tips and flight paths:** the quickest way to your next zone with hearthstone, flight paths, zeppelins and boats.
- **Your leveling report:** every level timed (AFK doesn't count), where the XP came from, gold and deaths, compared with your average (`/wp levels`). Weapon skill tips tell you when a weapon is falling behind and which weapon master teaches a new one.

## Gear for your class and spec
- **Upgrade scores on every tooltip,** weighted for your class and spec, with green arrows on upgrades in your bags and an Equip button when you loot one.
- **A gear guide for every class:** what to collect at each level and exactly where it comes from (dungeon and boss, or quest, zone and level).
- **2nd spec, any class, any spec:** its gear list, tooltip scores and a one-click gear swap.

## Professions, leveled the smart way
- **All twelve professions, 1-300,** with recipes, trainers, vendors and farming spots, and ore and herb alerts near a node that still gives skill.
- **What each step costs,** at vendor prices and your Auction House prices, with the cheapest recipe picked for you (one click switches to the one you can make from your bags). Ore, herbs and leather you gather yourself count as free.
- **The recipe's real colour and the game's own odds:** open your profession window and Waypath+ works out how many crafts a step really takes.
- **Shopping list** for this step, the next one or the rest of the route, with what you have (on this character and your others), what's left to get and what it costs. At a vendor, a **Buy profession materials** button appears; nothing is bought until you click it.
- **Where to get each recipe,** what you can make right now, your most profitable crafts, and tooltips that warn you before you sell a material your next steps need.

## Fishing, gold and bags
- **Fishing:** where to fish for your skill, poles and lures, a lure timer, fish per hour, and an immersive mode that fades your interface while you fish.
- **Auction House prices** from a background scan that never blocks your searches, remembered after you leave (Auctionator and TSM prices are used too). Items worth more on the AH than at a vendor are flagged and kept.
- **Bank alts, bag sorting, junk selling and repairs** at vendors in one click.

## The tracker
Your quests by zone with level colours, progress and distance; new quests nearby; your route; class quests, the next dungeon, your next upgrade and reminders in one tidy row. In a party: reminders for blessings and auras, alerts when someone needs a cleanse or is about to die, and a run report after every dungeon.

## Waypath Cards and the Arena
Every item, NPC, place and player you meet becomes a card with its own picture: over 1,300 cards to collect, in four finishes (Common, Rare, Holo and Full art) across seven rarities.
- **Booster packs earned by playing:** a free pack every day, a reward Jumbo every 7th day, packs for milestones, dungeons and quest lines, and coins from everything you do to buy more. Three wrappers (Pack, Jumbo, Super Jumbo); tear one open, and a glowing card back tells you something good is inside.
- **Six Mythic chase cards,** about one pack in 400, with their own painted art and a turning prismatic edge. The rarest of all, The 1 Ring, is one per account and engraved with the name of whoever opened it.
- **The Waypath Arena:** build a deck and duel the computer or a friend in your party, with classes, ranked seasons and a realm leaderboard. Show off your best pull in chat with one click.
- Not here to level? "Cards only" mode turns the guide off and keeps just the cards (`/wp cardsonly on`).

### Getting started
Install, log in, and a short setup asks how much help you want (Full guidance, Balanced or Minimal) and which professions you'll level, then a quick tour shows you around (`/wp tour` shows it again). Type `/wp` or click the minimap button; `/wp help` lists every command, and Settings has a search box.

### Bugs and ideas
`/wp report` gives you a copy-ready report with your last error and a link to the right form on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/nickflanders321-tech/RetGear/issues). `/wp share` sends us quest spots the add-on doesn't know yet, so the route keeps getting better.

### Language
Made in English. On German, French, Spanish and other game clients the arrow, tracker, route choice and professions work too; the add-on's own text stays in English for now, and a few sub-zone names still need an English client.
